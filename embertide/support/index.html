Embertide Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a feature idea? Email Scott.Robinson.Apps@outlook.com. Including your iPhone model, iOS version, and the app version (shown in Embertide's Settings) helps us fix things faster.

Common questions

Heart rate isn't showing up for a session

Embertide reads heart rate from Apple Health. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Embertide and make sure Heart Rate is allowed. If you wear a watch or ring from another brand, its app must sync heart rate into Apple Health first. If you've connected Oura, Embertide uses it as a fallback for manually entered sessions.

My sessions aren't syncing between iPhone and Apple Watch

Sync uses your private iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices and that iCloud is turned on in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Sync can take a few minutes, especially on a weak connection.

How do I disconnect Oura?

Open Settings → Integrations in Embertide and tap Disconnect. This deletes the stored sign-in token from your device.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Embertide. To remove workouts Embertide saved to Health, open the Health app and go to Profile → Apps → Embertide. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is Embertide medical advice?

No. Embertide is a tracker, not a medical device. Talk to your doctor before starting heat or cold therapy. See the Terms of Service.
