Scores you know. A game you'll actually play.

Recovery-aware training data meets the stuff that keeps athletes coming back: a scoreboard, a season and a team.

  • Recovery
  • Sleep
  • Strain

01

Four daily scores

Recovery, Sleep, Strain and Stress, read from Apple Health, with a clear look at how each one is calculated.

02

Strength, logged

Log sets on iPhone or Apple Watch, track PRs, and see which muscles are fresh and which need another day.

03

XP that respects recovery

Points are scored against what your body can handle today. On a low-recovery day, rest earns full credit.

04

Levels, badges, Seasons

Level up, collect badges, and chase quests across 8-week Seasons, each with its own recap at the end.

05

Weekly streaks

Streaks run week to week, so a rest day, a trip or a cold doesn't wipe out what you've built.

06

Squads & leaderboards

Private friend groups with boards for consistency, effort and strength progress, so everyone has something to win.

Your squad sees the score. Not your data.

Raw health data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. Friends only see the game stats you choose to share: XP, streaks, badges and PRs.
