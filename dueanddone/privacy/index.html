Due and Done Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Due and Done ("the app") is a household maintenance tracker developed by Scott Robinson ("we," "us"). It is available as an iPhone app and a web app. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • Your household's data lives on our server so everyone in your household can see the same tasks and history. It's protected in transit and only visible to members of that household.
  • Sign in with Apple only. We never see or store a password. You can use Apple's Hide My Email.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data selling. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Delete anytime. Delete your account in the app and we remove it right away, and remove what's left from backups within about 8 weeks.

What we collect and why

Your account

When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name (you can edit it), your email address (or an Apple private relay address if you choose Hide My Email), and a stable account identifier from Apple. We also store the token Apple gives us so we can revoke Sign in with Apple when you delete your account. We use these only to sign you in, show your name to your household, and contact you about your account if you contact us first. We don't send marketing email.

Your household's content

Everything you and your family enter is stored so the app can work and so it can be shared within your household:

  • Household settings: household name, time zone, units, reminder defaults.
  • Family members: display names, avatar colors, roles, away dates, and notification preferences. Parents can add managed members (for example, kids) with just a name; managed members have no login or email.
  • Assets: names and types of the things you maintain (home, pool, vehicles, RV, etc.), and any optional details you add, such as location in the house, make and model, serial number, VIN or license plate, and notes.
  • Meters and readings: odometer, engine-hour and similar readings you log, which we use to estimate when mileage- or hours-based tasks will be due.
  • Tasks and history: task titles, instructions, schedules, assignments, checklists and parts lists, plus each completion or skip with its date, who did it, readings, costs and notes, and an activity feed of household changes.

Don't put information in notes or details that you wouldn't want other members of your household to see.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your iPhone gives us an Apple push token so our server can send reminders through Apple Push Notification service. We store the reminders we send (title, text, related task) so they also appear in the app's notification list. We delete them after 180 days.

Kid view

If a parent pairs a device to a managed member's kid view, we store a hashed device token, an optional device label and when it was last used. That device can see and complete only that member's jobs. A parent can unpair it at any time.

Purchases

If paid features are offered, purchases on iPhone go through Apple and purchases on the web go through Stripe. We receive the purchase status and transaction identifiers so we can turn features on for your household. We never receive or store your card details.

Technical data

Like any website or online service, our server processes your device's IP address to deliver the app and to block abuse (for example, rate limiting). Server logs may briefly contain technical details about requests, which we use only for security and troubleshooting. The web app uses one essential sign-in cookie and stores your last-viewed household in your browser. It sets no advertising or analytics cookies.

What we don't collect

The app doesn't access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or Apple Health data, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see your data

  • Your household. Members of a household can see that household's assets, tasks, history and member names. Owners and admins can manage members and settings. Invite links and QR codes let anyone who has them join, so share them only with people you trust. Invites expire after 7 days and can be revoked.
  • Service providers we rely on to run the app: Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications, App Store purchases), the hosting company that runs our server, and Stripe for any web purchases. They process data on our behalf to provide these services.
  • When the law requires it. We may disclose information if we must to comply with a legal obligation, or to protect the safety and rights of users or others.

We don't sell or rent personal data, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles.

Retention and deletion

  • While you use the app, we keep your data so your household's history stays complete.
  • Deleting things: when you delete a task, asset or other item, it's removed from the app right away and permanently erased from our database after 30 days.
  • Deleting your account: go to Settings → Delete Account in the app. Your sign-in, account record and notification tokens are deleted immediately, and we revoke Sign in with Apple. Households you own where no one else has a login are deleted and permanently erased within 30 days. In households shared with others, the history you created stays visible to the remaining members under your display name, but it's no longer linked to any account. Email us if you'd like your name removed from that history.
  • Backups: we keep nightly database backups on our server for disaster recovery, rotated out after at most 8 weeks. Deleted data disappears from backups as they expire.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. Sign-in, invite, and kid-view tokens are stored hashed. The database isn't reachable from the internet. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify affected users if a breach affects their information as required by law.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your data in the app, export your history as CSV (where available), and delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Email Scott.Robinson.Apps@outlook.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We don't sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Children

Due and Done is meant for adults managing a household. It isn't directed to children under 13, and children can't create accounts. A parent or guardian may add a child as a managed member using a first name or nickname and may pair a device to kid view, so the child can see and check off their jobs. That information is provided and controlled by the parent, who can edit or remove it at any time. If you believe we have information about a child that a parent didn't provide, email us and we'll delete it.

Where data is processed

Our server is operated by a commercial hosting provider, and some services are provided by Apple and Stripe. Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where these providers operate.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we'll update this page and the effective date before the change takes effect. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
