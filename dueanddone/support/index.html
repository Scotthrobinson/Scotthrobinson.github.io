Common questions

How do I get started?

Sign in with Apple, name your household, then pick what you have, like the house, pool, cars, RV or generator. Due and Done adds suggested maintenance tasks for each one. You can change any interval or remove any task afterward. For mileage or engine-hour items, enter the current odometer or hour reading so the first due date is right.

How do mileage and engine-hour tasks work?

Log a reading from the asset's page, from the prompt on Today, or when you complete a task. Once you've logged two or more readings at least a week apart, Due and Done estimates your average daily use and shows an estimated due date (marked with a ~) alongside the distance left, for example "820 mi left · ~Nov 3". For "whichever comes first" tasks like oil changes, the earlier of the mileage and the date wins.

How do I invite my family?

Go to Family → Invite and tap Create Invite Link. Share the link or let them scan the QR code. They sign in with their own Apple Account and join your household. Invites expire after 7 days, and you can revoke them at any time.

Can I add my kids without giving them an account?

Yes. In Family, choose Add a Managed Member (No Login). Managed members need no email or login. You can assign them tasks and mark tasks done for them.

How do I set up kid view on a child's device?

On your phone, open the child in Family and tap Pair a Device to get a code. It's valid for 15 minutes. On the child's device, open Due and Done and tap Pair this device for a child on the sign-in screen, enter the code, and set an exit PIN. The child then sees only their own jobs. To leave kid view, tap Grown-ups: exit with PIN. If you turn on needs approval for a child, the jobs they finish wait for a grown-up to check them off.

How do rotations and "Anyone" tasks work?

In a task's assignment you can choose one person, a rotation (for example Leo, then Alex, then back to Leo, advancing each time it's done) or Anyone, where whoever gets to it first can claim it. You can also hand off just this week's turn without changing the rotation.

I'm going on a trip. Can someone cover my tasks?

Yes. Open yourself in Family, turn on Away Mode, set the dates, and choose who your tasks go to while you're away.

I'm not getting reminders
  • Check Settings → Notifications → Due and Done on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed.
  • In the app, check Settings → Notifications. If you chose Daily Digest, you get one summary at your digest time instead of separate reminders. Quiet Hours hold reminders until they end.
  • Reminders go to the person a task is assigned to (or to everyone for "Anyone" tasks), so a task assigned to someone else won't notify you.
  • The in-app notification list shows everything that was sent.
Can I use Due and Done on a computer?

Yes. A web version is available at dueanddone.50-6-21-36.sslip.io. Sign in with the same Apple Account to see the same household.

What does Due and Done Plus include, and how much is it?

Due and Done is free for up to 2 family members, 8 assets and 25 tasks. Plus covers your whole household: up to 8 members, unlimited assets and tasks, rotations and "Anyone" tasks, multiple households, kid view, the calendar, cost summaries and CSV export. Choose weekly (with a 3-day free trial), yearly, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Current prices for your country are shown in the app before you buy. One purchase covers everyone in the household.

How do I cancel, or restore a purchase?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone and choose Due and Done Plus, at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Settings → Plan & Export in Due and Done and tap Restore Purchases. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com, because Apple handles App Store billing.

What happens to my data if Plus ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your household goes back to the free plan, and everything you've added stays. Adding members, assets or tasks beyond the free limits, and Plus features like kid view and the calendar, need Plus again.

How do I export my history?

With Plus, go to Settings → Plan & Export, choose assets, tasks or history, and share or save the CSV file. You can open it in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account. If you own a household that other people with logins also use, you'll be asked to transfer ownership first. Deleting your account removes your sign-in immediately, revokes Sign in with Apple, and deletes households only you use. See the Privacy Policy for details, including backups.

Are the suggested intervals manufacturer-approved?

No. Templates are typical intervals to get you started. Always check your owner's manual and adjust the schedule to match it. See the Terms of Service.
